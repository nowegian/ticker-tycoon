# Ticker Tycoon

A stock-trading game inspired by *Wall Street Kid*. Great-Aunt Edna left you $100,000 and a challenge: hit her goals on time, outpace Cousin Victor, and buy the private island within three years of trading days to inherit her fortune.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

- 12 fictional companies on the Pinecrest Stock Exchange
- Morning paper, midday check-in and closing bell each trading day (NYSE-style calendar and holidays)
- Marty the broker, Delia the banker, Ray the realtor, Mr. Whitlock, Jordan and rival Victor
- Orders: buy/sell at open, limit, take profit, stop-loss, and auto-protect

Everything is in a single self-contained `index.html`; saves are stored in the browser.
